ABOUT THE PROJECT

Simple image classification.

DermaInsight AI is an academic prototype that classifies one image as Acne, Comedo, or Clear.

INPUTRGB image resized to 224 x 224
MODELEfficientNet-B0 CNN
OUTPUTOne dataset label and three softmax scores
EXPLANATIONGrad-CAM attention map

IMPORTANT LIMIT

Grad-CAM is a coarse explanation, not YOLO-style detection. It does not draw exact acne spots or bounding boxes. That would require lesion-level annotations and a separate detection model.
